Roll — Privacy Policy

App: Roll (Android phone and Wear OS), package com.bao.roll
Developer: baotrq
Contact: tdmbao2005@gmail.com
Last updated: 24 September 2026

Roll is a personal expense tracker. Your ledger lives on your phone. This page explains everything Roll reads, stores on your device, and sends off your device, and why.

Summary

  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data.
  • Your financial records are stored on your phone and are not uploaded unless cloud sync is turned on (not offered to new users in this version).
  • Two optional features send limited data off your device: voice speech recognition (via your phone's speech service) and automatic category suggestions for voice entries (via our server and Google Gemini).
  • Notification access, microphone and camera are optional. You can decline each and still use Roll.

This data is deleted when you delete it in the app, clear Roll's storage, or uninstall Roll. Android cloud backup is disabled for Roll. If you use Backup or Export CSV, Roll creates a file and hands it to the Android share sheet; where it goes after that is your choice.

Notification access (optional)

If you turn it on, Android lets Roll read the app name, title and text of notifications you receive. Roll uses this only to detect supported bank and wallet payment alerts and prepare an entry for you to review. Notifications from other apps, and alerts Roll does not recognise, are ignored and not stored. Notification content is processed on your device and is not sent to us. You can turn access off at any time in Android settings.

Microphone and speech recognition (optional)

Roll listens only while the voice-logging sheet is open. Speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service on your phone (for example Google's). Roll uses the on-device recogniser when available; otherwise your phone's speech service may process the audio over the internet under that provider's own privacy policy. Roll does not record, store or upload audio itself.

Automatic category suggestions

After a voice entry, Roll first tries to match a category on your phone. If it can't, it sends the entry's text note (for example "lunch"), whether it is income or expense, and your category names and IDs over HTTPS to Roll's server function hosted on Supabase, which asks Google's Gemini API to pick the best category. The amount, account, audio and your identity are not sent. We do not store the content of these requests. To prevent abuse, the server counts requests per IP address; that count and the IP address are deleted automatically within about 2 hours. Supabase and Google process them as service providers under their own terms, and may keep operational logs for a limited time. If you'd rather not use this, log entries with the keypad or camera instead.

Wear OS companion

If you pair the Wear OS app, entries and pending captures move between your phone and watch through Google Play services' Wear data layer. They are not sent to us.

Cloud sync (not available to new users)

Roll contains an optional sync feature that is not offered to new users in this version. If it is enabled for an account, the ledger (accounts, transactions, categories, budgets) and custom account images are uploaded over HTTPS to a Supabase database and storage, together with the account's email address and user ID. This policy will be updated, including how to delete an account and its cloud data, before sync is offered publicly.

Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. An optional fingerprint lock protects the app on your device. Roll does not request location, contacts, SMS, call logs or accessibility access.

Children

Roll is a personal finance app intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Your choices and deletion

  • Decline or revoke notification, microphone and camera permissions at any time in Android settings.
  • Delete any entry or photo inside Roll; uninstalling Roll removes all on-device data.
