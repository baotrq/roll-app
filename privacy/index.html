Roll · Expense tracker

Privacy Policy

Updated 7 Oct 2026 Android & Wear OS com.bao.roll

Roll is a personal expense tracker. Your ledger lives on your phone. This page explains everything Roll reads, stores on your device, and sends off your device, and why. Roll is developed by baotrq.

At a glance

  • ✓No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data.
  • ✓Your financial records are stored on your phone. They are uploaded only if you sign in and have Roll Pro, which backs your ledger and receipt photos up to our server so you can use Roll on more than one phone.
  • ✓You can delete your account, and everything stored with it on our server, from inside the app at any time.
  • !Some optional features send data off your device, each explained below: an account and Roll Pro's cloud backup (our server), purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat), voice speech recognition (your phone's speech service), category suggestions for voice entries (our server and Google Gemini), and exchange rates for other currencies (ExchangeRate-API).
  • ✓Notification access, microphone and camera are optional. You can decline each and still use Roll.

Data stored only on your device

Stays on phone
Accounts, balances, transactions, transfers, categories, budgets, savings goals, notesThe core ledger you create.
Receipt photos (camera or photo picker)Attached to an entry you choose. Roll uses the system photo picker and does not request broad storage access.
Payment notifications you haven't reviewed yetIf you grant notification access, supported bank/wallet alerts are parsed into a draft entry (amount, merchant, alert text) and kept until you confirm or dismiss it.

This data is deleted when you delete it in the app, clear Roll's storage, or uninstall Roll. Android cloud backup is disabled for Roll. If you use Backup or Export CSV, Roll creates a file and hands it to the Android share sheet; where it goes after that is your choice.

🔔

Notification access

Optional

If you turn it on, Android lets Roll read the app name, title and text of notifications you receive. Roll uses this only to detect supported bank and wallet payment alerts and prepare an entry for you to review. Notifications from other apps, and alerts Roll does not recognise, are ignored and not stored. Notification content is processed on your device and is not sent to us. You can turn access off at any time in Android settings.

🎙️

Microphone & speech recognition

Optional

Roll listens only while the voice-logging sheet is open. Speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service on your phone (for example Google's). Roll uses the on-device recogniser when available; otherwise your phone's speech service may process the audio over the internet under that provider's own privacy policy. Roll does not record, store or upload audio itself.

🏷️

Automatic category suggestions

Leaves phone
Note text + category names→Roll server (Supabase)→Google Gemini

After a voice entry, Roll first tries to match a category on your phone. If it can't, it sends the entry's text note (for example "lunch"), whether it is income or expense, and your category names and IDs over HTTPS to Roll's server function hosted on Supabase, which asks Google's Gemini API to pick the best category. The amount, account, audio and your identity are not sent.

We do not store the content of these requests. To prevent abuse, the server counts requests per IP address; that count and the IP address are deleted automatically within about 2 hours. Supabase and Google process them as service providers under their own terms, and may keep operational logs for a limited time. If you'd rather not use this, log entries with the keypad or camera instead.

⌚

Wear OS companion

If you pair the Wear OS app, entries and pending captures move between your phone and watch through Google Play services' Wear data layer. They are not sent to us.

☁️

Accounts, Roll Pro & cloud backup

Optional · leaves phone

An account is optional; Roll works fully without one. If you create one, we store your email address, a user ID and (if you sign up with email) a password, which our authentication provider keeps only as a secure hash. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and name. To confirm a new email address or reset a password we email you a one-time code. You can also choose your language for these emails, which we keep with your account.

With Roll Pro, Roll uploads over HTTPS and keeps on our server: your accounts and jars, transactions (amounts, currencies, notes, dates, categories), categories, budgets, people you split with and what you owe each other, repayment plans, custom jar images, your home currency, and your receipt photos and short receipt videos. Receipt photos upload only on Wi-Fi. Photos of people you add, your cat and game progress stay on your phone. Without Pro, nothing from your ledger is uploaded even when you are signed in.

This data is stored with Supabase (our database, file storage and sign-in provider) in Singapore and is visible only to your account. It is kept until you delete it in the app or delete your account.

💳

Purchases

Leaves phone

Roll Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check purchases: it receives your Roll user ID and your Google Play purchase records (product, price, dates, renewal and cancellation status), and tells our server whether your account has Pro.

💱

Exchange rates

Leaves phone

When you log an amount in another currency, Roll fetches the current exchange rate from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains only the currency code; like any web request it reveals your IP address to that service. No amounts or notes are sent.

🔒

Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. An optional fingerprint lock protects the app on your device. Roll does not request location, contacts, SMS, call logs or accessibility access.

🔞

Children

Roll is a personal finance app intended for adults and is not directed at children.

🗑️

Your choices & deletion

  • Decline or revoke notification, microphone and camera permissions at any time in Android settings.
  • Delete any entry or photo inside Roll; it is also removed from our server on the next sync. Uninstalling Roll removes all on-device data.
  • Delete your account in Roll › Profile & sync › Delete account. This removes your account and everything stored with it on our server — ledger, people, plans, receipt photos and videos — straight away; copies in our provider's backups expire within 30 days. A Google Play subscription is not cancelled by this: cancel it in Google Play. You can also ask us to delete your account without the app.
