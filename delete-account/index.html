Delete your account

Updated 7 Oct 2026 com.bao.roll

Roll is developed by baotrq. You can delete your Roll account, and everything stored with it on our server, at any time.

🗑️

In the app

  • Open Roll and tap your name at the top of the Yard to open Profile & sync.
  • Tap Delete account, then Delete.

Your account is deleted straight away. The ledger on that phone stays on the phone until you uninstall Roll.

✉️

Without the app

Email tdmbao2005@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Roll account”. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

📦

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, sign-in details, language and home currency.
  • Everything backed up with it: jars, transactions, categories, budgets, people and debts, repayment plans, jar images, receipt photos and receipt videos.
  • Your Roll Pro record on our server.

Nothing is kept, except copies inside our provider's backups, which expire within 30 days. Records of purchases stay with Google Play and RevenueCat as their own policies require. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.
